I constructed a criminal hunt puzzle for you. A murder happened. Someone with poor drawing skills tried to create a murder scene where Jane was killed.
Human investigators are struggling to decipher who the murderer is. There are five members at the murder scene. Police officer asks who saw the murder happen.
No one responds. Officer examines the body and asks each one - Abby, Andy, Amanda, Sandy and Bill.
The murder scene area adjacent to deadbody and is a 3 x 4 block is divided into three rows and four columns.
They are numbered starting from leftmost top square as 1 going left to right until 12 as rightmost bottom square, and filling numbers in the 3 x 4 rectangle sequentially.
Each numbered block is "visible" to only adjacent blocks - left, right, up and bottom squares adjacent to the current square.
Murder is only visible from the nearest row of the square. Here are the investigation responses.



Attached claims and square locations and claims by each one of the five suspects in 6 images.
first image is murder scene with the Police officer, five suspects and the corpse of Jane Doe.
Those who are at the nearest squares to the murder, could have seen the corpse and potentially have witnessed the murder or killed.
Others also could have murdered and went into squares to hide themselves. Can you help the police officer answer a question: who is not lying?

There is a murder scene. Corpse and 3 x 4 square numbered sequentially starting from leftmost top corner of the square.
Only adjacent cells are visible. Corpse is visible from 9,10,11,12 numbered cells.
Bill is in 9th cell, Andy is in 8th cell, Abby is in 4th, is this CONSISTENT or INCONSISTENT or CANNOT BE DETERMINED ?

Write down the claims from each person from the image and cell numbers visible.

Verify your answer to both questions:

1. who is not lying?
2. There is a murder scene. Corpse and 3 x 4 square numbered sequentially starting from leftmost top corner of the square. Only adjacent cells are visible.
Corpse is visible from 9,10,11,12 numbered cells. Bill is in 9th cell, Andy is in 8th cell, Abby is in 4th, is this CONSISTENT or INCONSISTENT or CANNOT BE DETERMINED ?

A puzzle about this puzzle : do you detect any logical inconsistencies in this puzzle? :)
