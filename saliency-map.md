This is a Saliency map for explainable image classification task using a Convolutional Neural Network, 
for visualization of explanations for an downstream image classification task trained on animal images. 
The saliency map, generated by the Explainable AI (XAI) framework, explains the regions that contributed to the correct prediction in identifying the animal.
The image is a saliency map for six animals classification task (map reads left to right) - dog, duck, zebra, bird 1, tiger and bird 2 - are visualized in this saliency map. 
Dog, zebra and tiger are animals that live on land. The two birds in this image are on the trees or are surrounded by leaves. 
And the duck image is swimming in the water. 
Each one of these animals are highlighted in 4 gradation of colors for specific features/regions - 1) red, 2) yellow, 3) aqua and 4) blue. 
Regions that are highlighted in red indicate most salient region that contributed to correct prediction of that animal/bird. 
And regions highlighted in blue indicate least salient region that contributed towards the prediction. 
Look at each animal image, focus on red regions in each image of the animal, identify which one of the following animal, 
feature text pairs are highlighted in red in the image, provide answer as an ordered list of numbers as answer.

1. dog - face
2. duck - neck
3. zebra - part of tail
4. bird 1 - face
5. tiger - tail
6. bird 2 - wings
